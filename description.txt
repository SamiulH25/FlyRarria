A living Terraria companion driven by real fruit-fly brain circuits.

A small mote follows you, gets hungry, startles at danger, grooms in the rain,
and dances when it trusts you. Its decisions come from extracted Drosophila
sensorimotor circuits (escape, steering, feeding, grooming, song) simulated as
leaky integrate-and-fire neurons — not from scripted AI.

Early scaffold (v0.1.0): pet follows/teleports, samples the world into a
SensoryFrame, steps the LIF circuits inline, and decodes descending-neuron
activity into movement. Full circuit data lands via tools/extract_circuits.py.
